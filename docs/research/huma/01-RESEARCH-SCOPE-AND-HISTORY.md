# 01 — Research Scope, Origins and Epistemic Separation

**Status:** Historical/conceptual synthesis; not an endorsement of predictive claims.

## 1. The questions in this discussion

The discussion began with the status of four traditional practices and expanded into a mathematical research program:

1. **Zi Wei astrology and physiognomy:** how calendars, cyclic signs and human appearance are used to construct traditional readings.
2. **I Ching (Yijing):** how Yin/Yang line patterns encode 8 trigrams and 64 hexagrams, and how symbolic transformations can be modeled.
3. **Numerology:** how digits, dates and names are mapped to symbolic labels and interpreted.
4. **Xuanxue, esoteric studies and broader 'mysticism':** how philosophical, religious and occult traditions historically sought hidden order, meanings and correspondence.
5. **Information theory, string diagrams, memory and AI:** whether a common formal language can preserve symbol structure, context, provenance, uncertainty and historical revisions.

Each tradition has its own textual history and internal interpretive conventions. Sharing symbolic words does **not** imply identical semantics, shared causation or scientific validity.

## 2. What is legitimate to formalize?

| Area | Legitimate mathematical object | What does **not** follow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yijing lines and hexagrams | finite binary strings; transition group; hypercube | that a reading predicts future events |
| Cyclic calendar notation | modular arithmetic; compatibility rules; calendar conversions | that a birth sign determines success or health |
| Numerology transforms | deterministic functions from dates/names to digits | that a numeric label causes personality |
| Physiognomy accounts | historically documented classification or textual assertions | that face shapes determine morality, intelligence or fate |
| Esoteric philosophies | typed concepts, texts, argument graphs and contexts | that unverifiable mechanisms exist in physics |
| AI memory | exact data, relational provenance, uncertain hypotheses and revisions | that correct bookkeeping guarantees true inference |

We preserve **distinctions** among: (a) mathematical theorem; (b) formal definition; (c) historical report of a belief; (d) empirically supported observation; (e) proposed empirical claim; (f) fiction or simulation. Provenance tracking must not silently promote an item between these categories.

## 3. Philosophy, interpretation and empiricism

A classic metaphysical or philosophical thesis can be studied by logic, textual criticism and conceptual comparison. A statistical claim about the observable world additionally needs a clearly measurable target, sampling assumptions, rival hypotheses and evidence. A computational encoding of a symbolic system establishes **computability of the encoding**, not the truth of the symbolism.

The research program therefore asks three separate questions:

- **Representation:** Can structures be described without loss of their declared formal relationships?
- **Memory:** Can an agent retain the original claims, their context, reasons and changes, including disagreement?
- **Evidence:** When someone makes a prediction, what would falsify it, and does it outperform matched alternatives?

The present continuation addresses the **first two at the level of theory only**. It intentionally does not run the third.

## 4. Why not identify HUMA with physical superstrings?

Physical string theory posits mathematical objects intended to describe fundamental physics. A HUMA *information string* or *string diagram* denotes a wiring/composition structure of information-processing morphisms. The shared word "string" does not establish a physical correspondence or quantum-mechanical explanation.

A future physical hypothesis would require an operational mapping, dimensional consistency, a distinct measurable prediction and a credible falsification path. No such hypothesis is asserted here.

## 5. Three ethical/technical boundaries

**Human categorization:** Traditional face readings and birth-based destiny claims must not become automated moral, employment, credit, medical or educational judgments about individuals.

**Cultural fidelity:** A source text and its interpretive tradition should be stored as source-attributed readings. A disputed interpretation should not overwrite an alternative.

**Research honesty:** Recording a theorem or a simulation as a claim is not equivalent to proving it or observing it. Experimental validity requires independent evidence outside the symbolic algebra.
